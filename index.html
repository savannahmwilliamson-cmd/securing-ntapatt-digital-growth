How we made it

A short Python script with a fixed random seed (2026) paired fictional first and last names and drew random IDs, products and amounts. Same seed, same five rows, every time.

What’s fake

Every field. Emails use example.com, a domain reserved for examples. Card digits come from public test card numbers. Maria’s name was picked by hand for the story.

Why mock data

We wanted records shaped like the real thing without exposing a single real customer. Showing that is the whole principle of this plan.

06 Our recommendation

Make every vendor prove it.

Vendor Management Program

Every outside company has to prove it protects our customers’ data. We start with vendors because that is where Maria’s data went three times in 90 seconds.

  • Security proof on file for every vendor
  • Contracts that require protection of customer data
  • Yearly reviews, sorted by risk tier
  • No new vendor goes live without review
Support 01

Give people less access

Role-based access, two-factor login, and summaries instead of full records.

Support 02

Keep less data

Label customer data, monitor cloud storage, and delete after one year of inactivity.

Support 03

Spot problems faster

Database logging, activity alerts and incident drills for 50% faster response.

08 Roadmap · 365 days

Four phases, each with an owner and a deadline.

Day 03090180365
Phase 1 · Days 0–30

Build the foundation

Inventory 100% of vendors and sort them into tiers. Adopt the rule that no new vendor goes live without review. Send Tier 1 questionnaires.

Vendor Mgmt & IT/Security

Phase 2 · Days 31–90

Lock down access and Tier 1

2FA with 15-minute auto-logout and least-privilege access live by day 60. All Tier 1 vendors reviewed, and database logging and alerts live, by day 90.

IT/Security & Managers

Phase 3 · Days 91–180

Open customer rights

Privacy portal and Tier 2 vendor reviews done by day 120. Incident drill and Tier 3 reviews done by day 180.

Legal/Compliance & Vendor Mgmt

Phase 4 · Days 181–365

Keep proving it

Yearly vendor re-review starts by day 330. 100% of vendors with valid proof on file by day 365.
