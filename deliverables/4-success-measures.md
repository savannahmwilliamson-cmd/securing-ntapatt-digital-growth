# 4. Success Measures: How We'll Know It Worked

**Customer Data Protection Program · NtapAT&T Digital**

Nov 17, 2026 · Savannah Williamson

## Purpose

The program succeeds if NtapAT&T Digital hits eight measurable targets by day 365, an independent audit confirms them, and spending stays within the $617,161 first-year budget. Each target covers one of the five risks in the risk register and has a single owner and a fixed deadline.

## Measures and targets

All eight measures are reported as a percentage against target, in order of deadline.

| Measure | Target | Deadline | Owner | Risk it addresses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vendors listed and sorted into risk tiers | 100% | Day 30 | Vendor Mgmt | Vendor leaks |
| Accounts on two-factor login | 100% | Day 60 | IT/Security | Cyberattacks |
| Accounts limited to role-based access | 95% | Day 60 | IT/Security & Managers | Excessive internal access |
| High-risk (Tier 1) vendors reviewed | 100% | Day 90 | Vendor Mgmt & IT/Security | Vendor leaks |
| Customer privacy requests met on time | 100% | From day 120 | Legal/Compliance & Marketing | Data misuse and privacy violations |
| Incident response speed, tested in a drill | 50% faster | Day 180 | IT/Security | Cyberattacks |
| Customer data stores labeled | 100% | Day 365 | IT/Data Management | Data lost or left unmanaged |
| Vendors with valid security proof on file | 100% | Day 365 | Vendor Mgmt & IT/Data Mgmt | Vendor leaks |

Open question: today's incident response time has not been measured yet. It is the baseline for the 50% target, so it needs to be recorded before Phase 2 ends.

## Phase checkpoints

| Phase | Checkpoint day | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Build the foundation | Day 30 | 100% of vendors listed and sorted into risk tiers |
| 2. Lock down access and Tier 1 | Day 90 | 2FA and role-based access live (day 60); all Tier 1 vendors reviewed; logging and alerts live |
| 3. Open customer rights | Day 180 | Privacy portal live (day 120); incident drill shows 50% faster response; Tier 2 and 3 reviews done |
| 4. Keep proving it | Day 365 | Data stores labeled; 100% of vendors with valid security proof on file |

A phase closes only when its checkpoint is met. The independent audit at day 365 confirms all eight targets.

## How we track and report

- **Monthly:** the program lead reports where each measure stands against its target, plus spending against the budget by phase.
- **Quarterly:** managers re-check their teams' access, so the 95% role-based target holds after day 60.
- **Missed target:** the owner names the cause and sets a recovery date at the next monthly report.
- **Budget:** $561,055 planned spend, plus a 10% contingency of $56,106 that is used only for overruns approved by leadership.
- **Year end:** an independent privacy and security audit confirms the results.

## What success means for the business

- **Finance:** lower exposure to fraud losses, fines, legal costs and recovery costs.
- **Sales:** customers keep access to their accounts and the trust that keeps them buying.
- **Marketing:** customer data stays accurate, consented to and safe to use in campaigns.
