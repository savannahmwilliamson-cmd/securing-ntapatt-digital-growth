# 2. Fix Plan + Roadmap: What to Do, in What Order

**NtapAT&T Digital · Securing Digital Business Growth**

## Recommendation: Vendor Management Program

Every outside company has to prove it protects our customers' data. We start with vendors because the top-ranked risk is there: Maria's data went to three partners in under a minute.

- Security proof on file for every vendor
- Contracts that require vendors to protect customer data
- Yearly reviews, grouped by risk tier
- No new vendor goes live without a review

Three supporting steps back the program up:

| | Step | What it means |
|---|------|---------------|
| 01 | Give people less access | Role-based access, two-factor login, and summaries instead of full records |
| 02 | Keep less data | Label customer data, monitor cloud storage, delete data after one year of inactivity |
| 03 | Spot problems faster | Database logging, activity alerts and incident drills, for 50% faster response |

## Risk mitigation plan

| Risk | Fix | Owner | KPI |
|------|-----|-------|-----|
| 1. Vendor leaks | Vendor Management Program: security proof, contracts, yearly reviews | Vendor Mgmt & IT/Security | 100% of high-risk vendors reviewed |
| 2. Cyberattacks | Two-factor authentication, rate limits, activity alerts | IT/Security | 100% of accounts on 2FA · 50% faster response |
| 3. Excessive internal access | Access by role, summaries not full records, approved AI tools policy | IT/Security & Managers | 95% of accounts limited |
| 4. Data misuse and privacy | Updated privacy notices, consent tracking, customer portal | Legal/Compliance & Marketing | 100% of requests on time |
| 5. Data lost or unmanaged | Label customer data, monitor cloud storage, delete after one year of inactivity | IT/Data Management | 100% of stores labeled |

## Roadmap: 365 days, 4 phases

```
Day 0      30          90                     180                                          365
|--P1--|-----P2-----|---------P3----------|-------------------P4---------------------------|
```

### Phase 1 · Days 0–30 · Build the foundation
- Inventory 100% of vendors and sort them into risk tiers
- Adopt the rule that no new vendor goes live without a review
- Send Tier 1 security questionnaires

**Owner:** Vendor Mgmt & IT/Security

### Phase 2 · Days 31–90 · Lock down access and Tier 1
- Turn on 2FA with a 15-minute auto-logout, and least-privilege access, by day 60
- Finish reviewing all Tier 1 vendors by day 90
- Turn on database logging and alerts by day 90

**Owner:** IT/Security & Managers

### Phase 3 · Days 91–180 · Open customer rights
- Launch the privacy portal and finish Tier 2 vendor reviews by day 120
- Run an incident drill and finish Tier 3 reviews by day 180

**Owner:** Legal/Compliance & Vendor Mgmt

### Phase 4 · Days 181–365 · Keep proving it
- Start the yearly vendor re-reviews by day 330
- Have valid security proof on file for 100% of vendors by day 365

**Owner:** Vendor Mgmt & IT/Data Management
