# 1. Risk Report: Top Risks, Ranked

**NtapAT&T Digital · Securing Digital Business Growth**

## Context

NtapAT&T Digital is growing across new markets and product lines. It has 1,000,000 registered customers and 500 employees. Its infrastructure, cloud services and customer data are growing faster than the controls, governance and resilience that protect them.

The company holds six kinds of customer data: personal information, loyalty program data, payment-related data, purchasing history, marketing data and support records. If that data is lost, misused, exposed or accessed inappropriately, the company faces financial loss, damage to its reputation, regulatory consequences and lost customer confidence.

**Example.** Maria (a fictional customer) buys a charging dock. Within seconds her data goes to three outside partners: payments (name, card, amount), email (name, email, order details) and analytics (purchase, loyalty tier). Every time data leaves the company, that's a risk.

## How risks were scored

Score = **Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5)**.

| Level | Score |
|-------|-------|
| High | 15–25 |
| Medium | 8–14 |
| Low | 1–7 |

## Ranked risks

| Rank | Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Level |
|------|------|----------|------------|--------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Vendor leaks | Third-party and vendor security | 4 · Likely | 5 · Severe | **20** | High |
| 2 | Cyberattacks on accounts and data | Cybersecurity and data breaches | 4 · Likely | 5 · Severe | **20** | High |
| 3 | Excessive internal access | Employee access control and insider threats | 4 · Likely | 4 · Major | **16** | High |
| 4 | Data misuse and privacy violations | Customer privacy and regulatory compliance | 3 · Possible | 4 · Major | **12** | Medium |
| 5 | Data lost or left unmanaged | Data management, retention and security | 3 · Possible | 4 · Major | **12** | Medium |

The top two risks tie on score. Vendor leaks rank first because customer data already goes to outside partners every time a customer buys something.

## Business impact

| Risk | Finance | Sales | Marketing |
|------|---------|-------|-----------|
| Vendor leaks | Fraud losses, legal expenses, fines and recovery costs | Customers may stop buying because they lose trust | Exposed customer information can damage customer relationships |
| Cyberattacks | Fraud losses, security recovery costs, legal expenses and fines | Customers may lose account access or avoid buying | Customer information used for marketing could be stolen or exposed |
| Excessive internal access | Financial losses, legal expenses and possible fines | Customer records and purchase data could be misused or exposed | Customer information could be shared without authorization |
| Data misuse and privacy | Regulatory fines, legal expenses and compliance costs | Customers may lose trust and buy less | Campaigns could be hit by consent restrictions and privacy violations |
| Data lost or unmanaged | Data recovery costs, storage expenses and financial losses | Missing customer records could interrupt purchases and customer service | Outdated or missing information could affect marketing campaigns |

Every one of the five risks hits finance, sales and marketing at the same time.
