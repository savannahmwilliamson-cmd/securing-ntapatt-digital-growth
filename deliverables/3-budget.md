# 3. Budget: Cost and Business Value

**NtapAT&T Digital · Securing Digital Business Growth**

## Year one costs $617,161

| Line | Period | Amount (rounded) |
|------|--------|------------------|
| Phase 1: Build the foundation | Days 0–30 | $76K |
| Phase 2: Lock down access and Tier 1 | Days 31–90 | $210K |
| Phase 3: Open customer rights | Days 91–180 | $149K |
| Phase 4: Keep proving it | Days 181–365 | $126K |
| **Planned spend** | | **$561,055** |
| Contingency (10% of planned spend) | | $56,106 |
| **Total, year one** | | **$617,161** |

The money pays for staff time, software licenses, outside services, training and equipment. Phase 2 costs the most because it's when two-factor login, least-privilege access, database logging and Tier 1 vendor reviews all go live. The contingency is used only for overruns that leadership approves.

The full line-item budget is in the [Customer Data Protection Roadmap Budget](https://claude.ai/artifact/7CmrbfbBz3ScAKJfQ6aLrP) sheet.

## Business value

**$0.62 per registered customer** for a full year of protection ($617,161 ÷ 1,000,000 customers).

Here's what that money protects against, for each part of the business:

- **Finance:** lower exposure to fraud losses, regulatory fines, legal costs and recovery costs.
- **Sales:** customers keep access to their accounts and keep the trust that keeps them buying.
- **Marketing:** customer data stays accurate, consented to and safe to use in campaigns.

A single vendor leak or cyberattack (both scored 20 out of 25) would hit all three teams at once. The program costs about 62 cents per customer to keep that from happening.

## The ask

Approve the Vendor Management Program and its $617,161 first-year budget. At year end, an independent privacy audit confirms the results.
